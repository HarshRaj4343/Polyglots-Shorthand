The model

A 4-block transformer over hashed word, alias and character pieces, plus a linear bag-of-features branch — distilled from hing-roberta-mixed and quantised to INT8 ONNX.

Known limits: negated negatives (“koi problem nahi hui”) are often wrong, the review flag rarely fires (thresholds calibrated to 0), and all evaluation data is synthetic. Every message is forced into one of the six intents.
